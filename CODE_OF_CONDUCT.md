# Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1. The full text is at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.

## Our standards

Contributors and maintainers keep the project a respectful place to work. This includes:

- Critique ideas and code on the evidence. Say what is wrong and why.
- Credit the work and the people behind it, and respect a decision once it is made.
- Keep discussion on the project. Do not publish other people's private information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, trolling, sexualized content,
publishing private information without consent, and any conduct that a reasonable person would
consider unprofessional in a technical setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break
these standards. They may block a person who repeats the behavior.

Report a concern by contacting the maintainer, [@landtml](https://github.com/landtml), on GitHub.
Reports are read in confidence. The maintainer decides on a response and tells the reporter the
outcome.
